## Physical-device checklist

After signing, verify certificate, checksum, source, package/version and in-place data preservation before installing over the normal highspec app. Do not install a debug variant or clear data.

1. Capture the actual Settings toggle and Player's next-title/reason card, with existing populated data and masked feed credentials.
2. Toggle off and restart the app to verify persistence, then restore the requested default-on state.
3. Exercise the end of an already-watched item, confirming a same-show unwatched continuation and a paused/disabled case. Avoid marking another unwatched source item complete merely for testing. Pause the successor promptly.
4. Verify the selected title follows the service's new item, saved incomplete progress is honored, fullscreen remains intact, and data/collapse choices remain available.
5. Exercise a background completion when practical. A real Cast receiver, changed physical font scales, and other devices require separate acceptance; do not infer these from JVM tests or a successful package build.

No live feed URL, authenticated response, or signing credential belongs in this report or committed test fixtures.
